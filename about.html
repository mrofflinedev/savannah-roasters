About Us

Our Story, Our Coffee, Our People

Our Story

Savannah Roasters is a proudly Kenyan coffee brand born in Nairobi. We started with a simple idea: Kenyans should enjoy the same fresh, high-quality coffee that we export to the world.

We work directly with smallholder farmers in the highlands of Kenya. We buy their best Arabica beans, roast them locally in small batches, and deliver them fresh to your cup.

For us, coffee is more than a drink. It is livelihood for farmers, pride for Kenya, and a daily moment of joy for you.

What Drives Us

Our Mission

To bring fresh, organic Kenyan coffee to every cup, while supporting our farmers fairly.

Our Vision

To be Kenya's most loved coffee brand, known for quality, fairness, and freshness.

Our Values

Fair Trade - Quality - Organic - Eco-friendly Packaging

From Our Farmers

Our coffee comes from smallholder farmers in Nyeri, Kirinyaga, Kiambu, and the Mt. Kenya region.

  • High altitude - Over 1,600m above sea level
  • Rich volcanic soil - Perfect for Arabica
  • Hand-picked - Only ripe cherries are picked
  • Fairly Paid - Farmers earn a fair price for their beans
