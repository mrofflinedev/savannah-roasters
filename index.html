Welcome to Savannah Roasters

We are a proudly Kenyan coffee brand from Nairobi. We work directly with smallholder farmers in the highlands to bring you fresh, organic Arabica coffee.

Every bag is roasted locally in small batches, so you taste the real flavour of Kenya - rich, smooth, and full of character.

Why Choose Us?

  • 100% Organic Kenyan Arabica
  • Fair Trade - Farmers paid fairly
  • Freshly Roasted Locally
  • Eco-friendly Packaging

From Farm to Cup

  1. Harvesting - Ripe cherries picked by hand
  2. Processing - Washed and sorted for quality
  3. Drying - Sun-dried on raised beds
  4. Roasting - Roasted in small batches in Nairobi
  5. Packaging - Sealed fresh in eco-friendly bags
